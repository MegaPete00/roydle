How to play

Roydle Rumble

Name the mystery Royal Rumble entrant in eight guesses.

The board

Every guess shows six numbers from that wrestler's Royal Rumble career, side by side with the mystery entrant's:

  • First Rumble and Last Rumble: the years.
  • Rumbles: how many Royal Rumble matches they were in.
  • Elims: total eliminations, all Rumbles added up.
  • Longest run: their longest time in a single Rumble.
  • Best finish: Won, 2nd, 3rd and so on.
  • Exact match
  • Close

Close means within 2 years on First and Last Rumble, 1 Rumble, 2 eliminations, 5 minutes on Longest run, and 3 places on Best finish. The word under a number tells you about the mystery entrant compared to your guess: an Older or More recent Rumble, More or Fewer Rumbles and eliminations, a Longer or Shorter run, a Better or Worse finish (Better means a higher spot on the card, closer to winning).

Who counts

  • Every men's Royal Rumble match from 1988 and every women's match from 2018, through the 2026 Royal Rumble. Each has its own daily.
  • The 2018 Greatest Royal Rumble in Saudi Arabia is not included.
  • One wrestler, one record. Different billings count as the same person: Cactus Jack, Mankind and Dude Love are all Mick Foley, and 1998 counts as one Rumble for him.
  • Wrestlers who never made it into the ring (no-shows, or taken out before their entrance) don't count. Whoever took the spot does.
  • Men's and women's are separate. Nia Jax, Beth Phoenix and Chyna also show up in the men's game with their men's numbers only.

Sharing

When you finish, copy your result to share it. "Copy for Discord" adds the answer behind a spoiler tag.

Where the numbers come from

Entrant-by-entrant Rumble data from Darren's Win-Loss Records. A few times and eliminations were corrected using WWE.com, Wikipedia and Smark Out Moment's Royal Rumble records, researched by Anthony Mango. Sources don't always agree on shared eliminations, so a total can differ by one or two from what you've seen elsewhere.
